A brief overview of my life right now, and what I’m working on.

This Website (lzon.ca)

I recently completed an overhaul of my /uses page. It’s already a very long page, and its expected to grow longer over time. I always knew I needed a good way to navigate the page. I had cobbled together a Table of Contents, which is provided as a feature of the static site generator that I use, called Zola. It worked but I wasn’t satisfied with it.

I’ve now removed the Table of Contents, and have replaced it with collapsible top-level headers for each section. It’s way easier to implement for me, and I think it’s better for the reader as well. When you click on that page, you are likely not wanting to read the entire page top-to-bottom. You will find something specific that you want to read e.g. Games. It’s just a single click or tap away, and there is no need for the visual clutter of a TOC.

Writing

Right now I’m interested in doing a piece about OpenGoal. It’s a project to port the Jak and Daxter series of games to PC. I’m a big fan of this series and have been tracking the project for several years. After a long wait the final game in the main trilogy (Jak 3) was finally added to the OpenGoal launcher, and I’ve really enjoyed playing that game again. I want to write about the history of the series itself, about the history of the OpenGoal project, and do a deep-dive into how OpenGoal works at a technical level.

Reading

I’m currently reading The Hobbit, by J.R.R. Tolkien. I read the Lord of the Rings Trilogy when I was in high school, following along with the cultural phenom that these stories had during the early 2000’s. I never read any other of Tolkien’s work before now, despite regarding myself as a lifelong fan. I’m liking it a lot, but the ending is surprisingly weak. I definitely think differently now about the Peter Jackson’s Hobbit trilogy. Fans complain that he added too much padding, which I still believe generally. But I totally understand wanting to make a better ending, at the very least.

My IT Services Company

I launched my IT services company on March 1, 2026. I’m proud of the work I did to pull that off, but the fair thing to say is that this business is not doing well. I’ve had a single customer (outside of friends or family), and I have some misgivings about it. I’m not giving up. My operating costs are very small so I’m willing to be patient, and work slowly towards finding customers that would need my services.

Employment

I’ve been applying to jobs since before I lost my previous one. My gut was telling me something was wrong so I didn’t hesitate to get my resume ready and submit applications.

By any reasonable measure my efforts as a job seeker have been a complete and utter failure. Literally the only response I could elicit from any company I’ve applied to was automated rejection emails, and even those have been rare. At this point I make every application expecting to be completely ignored.

I know that something is wrong, I just don’t know what. I have a very introverted personality, and have a lot of difficulty dealing with rejection. It makes sense to me that this is a relevant factor, but I don’t feel I have a clear answer, as of yet.

I am very privileged that I have enough support in my life that I don’t need to be producing an income right now. This can’t continue indefinitely, but I’m OK for now. I try to remain grateful.

Gaming

I have a few games that I’m playing at the moment.

I recently picked up Forza Horizon 5 during the Steam Spring 2026 sale. I already had Forza Horizon 4, and this is mostly the same game just set in Mexico instead of the UK. I like to call it ‘car-porn’. It has no sense of progression, but it also doesn’t expect anything from you (e.g. it has no story to get invested in). I like to pick it up here-and-there and just go for a drive. vroom vroom.

My brother and I are both huge fans of the Monster Hunter series, and have been recently playing the latest title, called Monster Hunter Wilds. The game does not perform well, at least not on mainstream gaming PC configurations. Performance has improved since launch but it is still not to my preference even after I tweak the settings. The game is well designed though, so I’m willing to overlook the performance issues. I’ve been enjoying playing it.

Every day almost as a ritual, I play Tetris: The Grand Master 2. I will admit to being a bit of a Tetris enjoyer, and TGM2 is the version that suits me best. The exact combination of gameplay elements just works perfectly for me.

Touching Grass

The weather is starting to get warm in Southern Ontario. We had a harsh winter this year and the cold temperatures are lingering. I saw snow this morning, which is unusual for early April in this part of the world.

At the end of last year I became interested in drone flight. I currently have a DJI Mini 4K. I’ve only had a precious few opportunities to fly through the winter. The weather is (slowly) getting warmer, so it won’t be very long before I can get outside with it again. The video recordings I can make with it are astounding, and everyone who’s seen them has been as amazed as I was. Beyond that I just really enjoy flying it, I find modern quad-copter technology absolutely fascinating.
