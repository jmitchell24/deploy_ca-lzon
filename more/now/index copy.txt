### Algoma Programming Language

This is the current object of my attention. It's a programming language that I'm trying to build from scratch. I'm using the excellent [Crafting Interpreters](https://craftinginterpreters.com/) by Robert Nystrom as a guide. I'll be deviating from the book as I go. My code for the project is written in C++ (instead of C), and I won't be supporting an object-oriented programming model. Overall I expect my language to be very Lua-like. 

If you are interested you can [click here](/posts/manuscripts/algoma4) to see my post about it. I have a WASM build of the Algoma editor that works directly in the browser.

### My IT Services Company

I launched my IT services company on March 1, 2026. I'm proud of the work I did to pull that off, but the fair thing to say is that this business is not doing well. I've had a single customer (outside of friends or family), and I have some misgivings about it. I'm not giving up. My operating costs are very small, so I'm willing to be patient, and work slowly towards finding customers that would need my services. 

### Employment

I've been applying to jobs since before I lost my previous one. My gut was telling me something was wrong, so I didn't hesitate to get my resume ready and submit applications. 

By any reasonable measure my efforts as a job seeker have been a complete and utter failure. Literally the only response I could elicit from any company I've applied to was automated rejection emails, and even those have been rare. At this point I make every application expecting to be completely ignored. 

I know that something is wrong, I just don't know what. I have a very introverted personality, and have a lot of difficulty dealing with rejection. It makes sense to me that this is a relevant factor, but I don't feel I have a clear answer, as yet. 

I am very privileged that I have enough support in my life that I don't _need_ to be producing an income right now. This can't continue indefinitely, but I'm OK for now. I try to remain grateful.
