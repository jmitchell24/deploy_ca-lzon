Nibble

You probably already know that a byte is 8 bits, but have you ever heard of a ‘nibble’? A nibble is 4 bits. A few older chip architectures used it, but modern CPU’s typically use a 64-bit word length.

It’s not particularly relevant anymore. I personally had to use nibbles when I was writing my Gameboy emulator.
