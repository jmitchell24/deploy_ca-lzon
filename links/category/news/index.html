Star Citizen has reached $1 billion in funding

The Star Citizen project has now received over one billion dollars in funding. The game was initially slated for release in October 2014, after being revealed at GDC in October 2012. As of today it has been over 13 years since the announcement, which is nearly 5000 days.

This is my take: These funding milestones hardly count as news anymore. The game has a loyal fan-base who are playing the game regularly, and are committed to supporting the game financially on an on-going basis.

The game has long since been ‘released’, for all intents and purposes. To say that it is still in early access is purely a function of marketing.

Starbucks scraps AI inventory tool across North America

Starbucks is ending the use of an AI-based inventory manager that it began using in September 2025. The software was making too many mistakes, which included miscounting and mislabeling items. This decision comes after a multi year effort by CEO Brian Niccol to increase efficiency of the company’s supply chain.

The developer of the software NomadGo said in a statement to Reuters that it is, “continuously learning from customers and user feedback” to improve its products.

To me this appears to be a signal that the AI hype bubble is coming to an end but as John Maynard Keynes famously said

“the market can remain irrational longer than you can stay solvent”

Steam Controller to have a reservation queue

Starting May 8th, Valve will implement a reservation queue for the Steam Controller.

These are the details:

  • You will have 72 hours to make the purchase on Steam.
  • Customers who have already purchased a Steam Controller are not eligible to reserve another.
  • Your account must be in good standing on Steam
  • You must have made a purchase on Steam prior to April 27, 2026

The Steam Controller went on sale on Monday, May 4th an sold out almost immediately.

This reminds me of the chaos during the initial launch of the Steam Deck back in 2022. It took Valve months to clear the reservation list for the handheld, hopefully it won’t take that long this time.
