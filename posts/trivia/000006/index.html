Trivia 6
Posted October 26, 2026 (Updated November 12, 2026) ScienceAnthropology Random Trivia

Among all species Humans are one of the best at long-distance endurance running.

A noteworthy ability of the Human species is to run for long, uninterrupted periods of time. Few species are better, and Humans are the only ape that is capable of it.

Among many others, specific traits which enable this include:

  • Taller body with more surface area which facilitates evaporative cooling (sweating)
  • Wider heel bone to better absorb impact
  • Longer legs enabling a greater stride

It is an ability that likely evolved in conjunction with Persistence Hunting, a technique which involves chasing an animal over long distances. The Human hunter can withstand the chase better than their prey, and eventually they will catch it even if initially the animal can run faster. If the hunter can keep chase long enough, the prey animal will either succumb to exhaustion, overheating or injury.
