Homescreen Mantras
 Posted February 13, 2026 4 min read

The mantras that I remind myself of every day.

For a long time I’ve had a list of mantras that I keep on my phone. They are displayed in a widget on my home-screen, so that I can see them every time I unlock my device. This hasn’t made a night-and-day difference in my life, but it hasn’t been worthless either. Even if it is just a little bit of good life advice, I appreciate and make use of these little reminders.

The following is the list of my current home-screen mantras.

“If you create something, write about it.”

Can you tell that I’ve been taking this one seriously? I made an entire website from scratch and won’t shut up about it!

I think I’ve always enjoyed writing. I’ve kept a personal journal off and on for many years, and otherwise just enjoyed the mental exercise of ‘word-crafting’. I feel that writing can be a very good form of self reflection. It forces you to organise your thoughts, to give them structure. This is very much a good thing if you are thinking deeply about your own mind.

For myself specifically, I have tried to build a habit of writing about the things that I create. I am both quite introverted and project-oriented. My entire adult life I’ve always had something that I’ve been building: custom computers, servers, video games, programs, and more. I’ve always felt my work (some of it, at least) deserved better than to just sit in my /projects folder until it succumbs to bit rot. It is my fear of judgement that prevented me from sharing any of it with others.

I still struggle with this, so I make sure to remind myself to share what I create.

“Make eye contact. It works.”

This is another mantra that I need to keep on my phone as a reminder. My social skills have always been quite below average. I have struggled with making consistent eye contact my entire life, and even now after gaining so much confidence in my speaking ability I still have to force it. It’s frustrating for sure, but I know it that it matters, so I make sure that I don’t forget to do it.

“Make others smile, or laugh.”

I really like this one, its both easy and satisfying. Smile and you will receive one back. Expressing pleasantness to others is almost always reciprocated, which is great.

My opportunities to express humour are somewhat more rare, but every one-in-a-while I’ll see the opportunity, seize the moment, and get a laugh out of someone. It’s great when it happens, for everyone involved.

“Be the person you needed.”

This is the most important one for me. I’ve had some major struggles in my life, and (rightly or wrongly) I do feel as though I didn’t receive the support that I needed at the time. I feel that never having that support changed my life path for the worse.

I think this mantra is the best possible response to those feelings. I’m not at all consistent about this one, but it forces me to least try to identify opportunities to help others, in the ways that I had always wished others had helped me.

These would be some examples:

  • If I haven’t spoken to a friend in a while, I know I should reach out just to say ‘hi’.
  • If a family member has stopped responding to messages, I know I should knock on their door to make sure they are OK.
  • If someone has just had a major accomplishment, I know that I need to congratulate them, to acknowledge their success.

Wrapping Up

If you’ve read this far into my post, thank you very much. I haven’t written much for lzon.ca lately. I’ve been spending a lot of time on the website itself. It’s very rewarding, and the site is better for it, but I did feel that I wanted to get back to writing.
