Study demonstrates potential anti-aging effect in GLP-1 drugs

Researchers from the University of California analyzed data from a recent study to determine if semaglutide had anti aging effects. They discovered that study participants who received the drug had non-trivially improved markers of biological aging.

As with any study of this kind, It’s difficult to untangle the direct effects of the drug and the lifestyle improvements that it encourages. I myself lost a lot of weight without medical intervention, just a lot of personal effort and a change in diet. My bias is to believe that for most people this is enough, and that becoming dependent on a pharma corporation for your health should be avoided.

Micro Center now lists GeForce RTX 5090 cards as 'Not Carried'

The American computer retailer Micro Center is indicating that they are no longer receiving new RTX 5090 graphics cards from any of their suppliers. This follows from a recent rumor that NVIDIA stopped supplying any of their GB202 chips for consumer cards. The GB202 being the chip that is used in the RTX 5090.

Without an official statement from NVIDIA the obvious assumption to make is that they want to allocate all GB202 chip production to enterprise cards, since those can be sold for a much higher margin.

I think this is a consequence of the massive capital spending on AI data centers. I’m not a financial expert but at surface level it very much seems as though these investments are irrational and will have sweeping economic consequences once the price correction occurs.

And as a gamer and PC enthusiast this is very disappointing. While not the end-all-be-all of the hobby, it was nice being able to build and upgrade relatively powerful PC’s for affordable prices. For the time being at, least seems to be no longer possible.

Earth reaches farthest orbit from Sun for 2026

The Earth’s orbit is not quite circular, which means that through each year there is a point at which the Earth is closest and farthest from the Sun.

The farthest distance is referred to as ‘Amphelion’, and the shortest is called ‘Perihelion’.

Perihelion was reached back in January, and today the Earth will reach it’s Amphelion (farthest distance) for this year.

Valve launches the Steam Machine starting at $1049 USD.

Valve has officially launched the new Steam Machine. It’s a small gaming PC intended to provide a more console like, living room oriented experience. It comes preinstalled with Steam OS, the same linux-based operating system used on the Steam Deck.

The device was originally announced near the end of last year and has been delayed due to DRAM and flash chip shortages caused by the AI economic bubble. Valve has stated that they were struggling to procure the parts they needed, and that it wasn’t merely a matter of increased prices.

Speaking of price, the cheapest model with 512GB of storage will cost $1049 USD, with higher storage variants costing several hundred dollars more. Valve has said they increased pricing since the announcement in line with the recent Steam Deck price increases. This implies that the original MSRP would have been in the range of $750.

My opinion is that this is overall a good but expensive product. It is a device intended to create the most polished, cohesive, and easy experience for PC gaming. But given the current price, and even its original MSRP, this can’t really be a mass market product. I think a lot of people who would have otherwise wanted one will be unable to afford it. That’s unfortunate of course, and hopefully discounts will be possible in the future.
